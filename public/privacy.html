Privacy Policy — Shadow Depths

Last updated: July 2026

Shadow Depths is an offline-first turn-based roguelike. We do not operate user accounts in this version of the app. Game progress is stored locally on your device.

Data we collect

  • Local storage: save games, settings, and meta-progress are kept in your browser or app storage on your device.
  • No personal data required: you can play without providing name, email, or phone number.

In-app purchases

The Android app offers an optional one-time unlock (“Full Descent”) processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase entitlement is remembered on your device and can be restored after reinstall via Google Play. Contact Google for payment disputes; contact us for app entitlement issues.

Network use

The mobile app runs the game from bundled files and does not require internet to play. Internet is used only when you purchase or restore the Full Descent unlock (Google Play), or when you visit our website. Standard connection logs may be collected by your host, CDN, or Google Play.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via the GitHub repository: fareza777/Shadow-Depths.
